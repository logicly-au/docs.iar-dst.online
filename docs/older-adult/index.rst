Older Adult (65 years and over)
=================================

  

This documentation provides extracts from relevant sections of the
:download:`National Guidance - Initial Assessment and Referral for Mental Health Care; Part E - Older Adult (65 years and over) </other-resources/IAR Guidance for Mental Health Part E - Older Adults v1.1.pdf>` which has been designed to assist general practitioners and clinicians to recommend the most appropriate level of care for an older adult (aged 65 years and over) seeking or requiring mental health support.

Whilst the Guidance uses age to indicate the overall appropriateness of each tool, the final decision about the most appropriate version is based on the clinical judgment of the user, taking into account contextual and developmental considerations.
